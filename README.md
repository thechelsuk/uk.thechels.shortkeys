## What is ShortKeys?

ShortKeys is a private, on-device text expander for iPhone and iPad. Write a
snippet once — a sign-off, an address, a canned reply, a whole paragraph — and
bring it into any conversation with a few taps. From a quick sign-off to a full
message, your most-used words stay close by.

## Features

- **Multi-line text expansion** — expand a short trigger into anything from a word to a full paragraph.
- **Works across your apps** — a system keyboard that drops your text into every app you use.
- **Favourites, one tap away** — star up to three favourites for a one-tap bar above the keyboard.
- **Stay organised** — tags, pinned collections, and search to find any snippet fast.
- **iCloud sync** — your ShortKeys and keyboard preferences sync across your devices.
- **Keyboard that feels at home** — haptics, keyboard sounds, auto-capitalisation, and a double-space period.
- **One-backspace undo** — undo an expansion and get back exactly what you typed.
- **Automation** — trigger ShortKeys with deep links and Apple Shortcuts.
- **Backup & restore** — export and restore your library as JSON.
- **Light, Dark & System** appearance with a tint to match your style.

## FAQ

**What is a text expander?**
A text expander lets you type a short trigger (like `addr`) that instantly
expands into longer text (like your full postal address), saving you from
typing the same things over and over.

**Does ShortKeys work in every app?**
Yes. ShortKeys is a custom iOS keyboard, so your snippets are available in any
app where you can type.

**Is my data private?**
Yes. Everything stays on your device and in your personal iCloud account. No
data is collected by the developer.

**Which devices are supported?**
iPhone and iPad running iOS 26.1 or later.
